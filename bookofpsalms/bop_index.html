Frequently Asked Questions
How long does delivery take? ▼
Standard delivery takes 5–10 business days after order confirmation. We will notify you once your book is shipped.
Can I change my child's photo after ordering? ▼
Once your order is confirmed and in production, we are unable to change the photo. Please double-check your photo before confirming.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept GCash and bank transfer. Payment instructions are provided after you click Order Now.
Is the photo of my child safe? ▼
Yes. Your child's photo is used only to create your personalized book and is never shared with third parties. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I order as a gift? ▼
Absolutely! Many of our books are ordered as baptism gifts, birthday gifts, and Christmas gifts. You can customize the dedication message for any occasion.
