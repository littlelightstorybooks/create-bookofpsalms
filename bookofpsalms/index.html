✦ Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take? +
Standard processing is 3–5 business days after payment confirmation, plus shipping time depending on your location.
What is the maximum name length? +
Up to 8 letters at the standard price. Each additional letter is charged separately. Spaces are free.
Can I give this as a gift? +
Absolutely! The Little Book of Psalms makes a beautiful gift for baptisms, birthdays, baby showers, and dedications.
What photo should I upload? +
A clear, well-lit photo of your child's face works best. Landscape orientation is ideal. JPG or PNG up to 10MB.
